Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

SnapTag Arena ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By downloading, installing, or using SnapTag Arena, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Age Requirements

SnapTag Arena is available to players aged 13 and over. Players under 18 may participate in games and redeem coins for merch prizes only. Cash payouts via PayPal require players to be 18 years of age or older. By using the Service, you confirm you meet the applicable age requirement for the features you use.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your email address, username, and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile information: Your chosen username, tier (Free, Basic, or Pro), and coin balance.
  • Payment information: Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card details. We receive confirmation of payment and subscription status from Stripe.
  • PayPal email address: If you request a cash payout (18+ only), we collect your PayPal email address to process the payment.
  • Merch claim information: If you redeem coins for merch, we collect your name, email, size preference, and shipping address to fulfil your order.
  • Device information: Device push notification tokens to deliver real-time tag alerts and game notifications.
  • Usage data: Game participation history, survive events, elimination events, and coin transactions.
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, operating system, and app version for troubleshooting and service improvement.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Operate the game and process game events in real time
  • Process subscription payments and cash payout requests (18+ only)
  • Fulfil merch prize orders
  • Send push notifications for tag alerts and game updates
  • Maintain leaderboards and game history
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Improve the app and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication services. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: For payment processing. See Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • PayPal: For cash payout processing when you request a withdrawal (18+ only).
  • Printify: For merch fulfilment. Your name and shipping address are shared with Printify to process merch orders.
  • Google Cloud: Our backend infrastructure runs on Google Cloud Run and Cloud SQL.
  • Law enforcement: If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Your username and leaderboard ranking are visible to other players within the app.

5. Children's Privacy

SnapTag Arena is available to users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we become aware that a person under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

Users aged 13–17 may participate in games and redeem coins for merch prizes. Cash payouts are restricted to users aged 18 and over. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child's use of the Service, please contact us.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance purposes.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), secure authentication via Firebase, and encrypted database storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain types of data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Push Notifications

We use push notifications to alert you when you are tagged in a game. You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings. Disabling notifications may affect your ability to respond to tags in time.
